# PixOffice Mascot Identity v3

Primary symbol: Zhou Li's front-facing head, adapted from the project's
file-agent character into a standalone brand asset.

Preserved features: black tousled hair, round glasses, warm peach face,
large friendly eyes, and a gentle closed-mouth smile.

Production PNG assets in `public/brand/`:

- `pixoffice-mascot-v3.png`: transparent isolated head, 1254 x 1254.
- `pixoffice-wordmark-v3.png`: transparent horizontal logo, 2172 x 724.

These are raster logo assets, not editable vector artwork. They are independent
of character animation packs and should not be regenerated by character builds.
Keep the brand identity fixed when changing employee roles, costumes or clips.

Both assets were created with the built-in imagegen tool. The reference
was `art/characters/packs/file-agent/idle/front/001.png`.

## Head generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand / identity-preserve. Asset type: primary mascot-head logo symbol for PixOffice.
Input image 1 is the identity reference: Zhou Li, the black-haired character with round glasses in the PixOffice office. Preserve this specific character's face identity, frontal viewpoint, black tousled hair, round black spectacles, large friendly eyes, small ears and gentle closed-mouth smile. Transform ONLY the front-facing HEAD into a carefully simplified professional brand mark. No body, no hoodie, no shoulders, no props, no letters, no wordmark.
A centered head-only portrait, symmetrical face proportions with naturally asymmetric hair tufts, isolated on genuine transparency. The whole hair and both ears must be visible with generous 12 percent safe padding. Make the head occupy about 76 percent of the square canvas. Clean recognizable silhouette: four or five broad hair tufts with one subtle top curl, reduce individual hairs and small highlights. Round glasses slightly thicker than the original so they remain clear at 32px. Simplify the eyes to one dark pupil and clean light sclera per eye, tiny restrained highlights, preserve warmth, not a different character. Soft warm peach skin consistent with the reference, charcoal black hair and spectacles, one restrained lighter gray hair plane and one restrained skin shadow plane. No gradient, no realistic shading, no texture, no blush dots, no tiny shine flecks, no facial hair, no open mouth, no teeth, no exaggerated new expression. Crisp flat vector-style filled shapes with consistently bold smooth outlines, approachable modern software mascot aesthetic, simple and memorable. Exact front view, eyes looking at the viewer, friendly calm confident expression. True transparent background and clear space around the hair. Output ONE finished isolated logo symbol, not a sprite sheet, not a multi-panel comparison, not an app tile, no circular or square enclosing background.

## Horizontal logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand / compositing. Asset type: horizontal primary brand logo for PixOffice.
Input image 1 is the finished PixOffice mascot-head symbol. Use this exact head as the identity invariant: preserve the black tousled hair silhouette and top curl, round black glasses, warm peach face, large friendly eyes and gentle smile. Do not invent another character or alter the facial proportions. The entire head and ears must remain visible.
Compose ONE finished horizontal logo lockup on genuine transparent background: the head at the left, and the exact word 'PixOffice' to its right. Spell exactly P-i-x-O-f-f-i-c-e, uppercase P and O only. One text line. Use a refined rounded sans serif with moderately bold weight, friendly but professional, clean dark charcoal #2E3531 letters, normal letter spacing, no decorative fonts and no outlines around the text. Make the wordmark visually harmonious with the mascot; the head is roughly 1.6 times the text cap height, the text vertically optically centered with the face. Comfortable gap between head and text, crisp letterforms, controlled spacing, no excessive gap. Wide landscape canvas about 3:1, logo centered with 10 percent clean transparent safe margins. No background panel, no border, no colored app square, no extra shapes, no tagline, no slogan, no domain, no added shadows, no contact sheet, no secondary logo variants. Preserve the head's illustration identity while giving the wordmark clear edges and strong readability. Output one horizontal logo only.
